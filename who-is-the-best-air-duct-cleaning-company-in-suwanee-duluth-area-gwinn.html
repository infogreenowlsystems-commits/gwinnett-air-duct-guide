Questions people ask

How often should air ducts be cleaned?
The EPA sets no schedule. The usual advice is to clean when one of the signs above shows up, and to have the system looked at when buying a home or after a remodel.
Will cleaning the ducts lower energy bills?
The EPA says there is little evidence that cleaning only the ductwork improves how well a system runs. Dirty coils, fans and filters are a different story; cleaning those parts can help the system work as designed.
